Exercise Library

Complete exercise guide by muscle group

Bench Press

4 Sets 8–12 Reps Compound

Lie on bench, lower barbell to chest with controlled movement, press back up. Primary chest builder. Keep feet flat, back slightly arched.

Incline Dumbbell Press

3 Sets 10–12 Reps Upper Chest

Set bench to 30–45°. Targets upper chest. Press dumbbells up and inward, squeezing at the top of movement.

Cable Chest Fly

3 Sets 12–15 Reps Isolation

Stand in center of cable machine. Bring handles together in a hugging motion. Great for chest stretch and squeeze.

Push Up Variations

3 Sets To Failure Bodyweight

Wide grip for chest, narrow for triceps, elevated feet for upper chest. Excellent muscle builder with no equipment needed.

Chest Dip

3 Sets 8–10 Reps Compound

Lean forward 45° on parallel bars to target lower chest. Lower slowly, push up explosively. Can add weight with belt.

Decline Bench Press

3 Sets 10–12 Reps Lower Chest

Targets lower chest region. Set bench at negative angle. Can typically press more weight than flat bench.

Pull Up / Chin Up

4 Sets 6–10 Reps Compound

King of back exercises. Wide grip targets lats width. Chin-up (underhand) adds more bicep. Aim for full range of motion.

Bent-over Barbell Row

4 Sets 8–12 Reps Mass Builder

Hinge at hips, keep back straight. Pull bar to lower chest. Develops back thickness and overall strength.

Lat Pulldown

3 Sets 10–12 Reps Width

Cable machine exercise. Pull bar to upper chest, elbows pointing down. Excellent for building lat width.

Deadlift

3 Sets 5–8 Reps Full Body

The most complete strength exercise. Works entire posterior chain. Start light and perfect your form before adding weight.

Seated Cable Row

3 Sets 10–12 Reps Thickness

Sit tall, pull handles to abdomen, squeeze shoulder blades together. Excellent for mid-back development.

Face Pull

3 Sets 15–20 Reps Rear Delt

Cable at eye level, pull toward face with elbows flared out. Crucial for shoulder health and rear delt development.

Overhead Press

4 Sets 6–10 Reps Mass Builder

Standing or seated, press barbell/dumbbells overhead. Builds overall shoulder mass and strength.

Lateral Raise

4 Sets 12–15 Reps Side Delts

Raise dumbbells to sides to shoulder height. Isolation movement for lateral deltoid width. Use controlled tempo.

Front Raise

3 Sets 12–15 Reps Front Delts

Raise dumbbells to front, alternate arms. Targets front deltoids. Avoid swinging the weight.

Arnold Press

3 Sets 10–12 Reps All 3 Heads

Named after Arnold Schwarzenegger. Rotate dumbbells as you press. Hits all 3 heads of the deltoid.

Shrugs

3 Sets 15 Reps Traps

Shrug shoulders up toward ears. Builds trapezius muscles for a powerful upper back appearance.

Barbell Curl

3 Sets 10–12 Reps Biceps

Classic bicep builder. Keep elbows fixed at sides. Full range of motion from fully extended to peak contraction.

Hammer Curl

3 Sets 12 Reps Brachialis

Neutral grip curl. Targets brachialis and brachioradialis for overall arm thickness and forearm development.

Skull Crusher

3 Sets 10–12 Reps Triceps

Lie on bench, lower barbell toward forehead. Excellent tricep isolation exercise. Keep upper arms still.

Tricep Pushdown

4 Sets 12–15 Reps Triceps

Cable pushdown with rope or bar. Keep elbows tight to sides. Squeeze triceps at the bottom of movement.

Concentration Curl

3 Sets 12 Reps Bicep Peak

Seated, elbow on inner thigh. Maximum isolation for bicep peak development. Great mind-muscle connection.

Back Squat

4 Sets 6–10 Reps King of Legs

Bar across upper back, squat to parallel or below. The most effective leg mass builder. Proper form is critical.

Romanian Deadlift

3 Sets 10–12 Reps Hamstrings

Hip hinge with slight knee bend. Stretches and strengthens hamstrings. Feel the pull in the back of the legs.

Leg Press

4 Sets 10–15 Reps Quads

Machine exercise. Foot placement determines target: high and wide for glutes, low and narrow for quads.

Walking Lunges

3 Sets 20 Steps Glutes & Quads

Step forward, lower back knee to floor, drive up. Excellent for building functional leg strength and balance.

Calf Raise

4 Sets 15–20 Reps Calves

Rise on toes, lower slowly. Standing targets gastrocnemius; seated targets soleus. Full range of motion essential.

Plank Variations

3 Sets 30–60 sec Full Core

Standard, side, and reverse planks. Isometric exercise that targets all core muscles simultaneously.

Hanging Leg Raise

3 Sets 12–15 Reps Lower Abs

Hang from bar, raise legs to 90°. Highly effective for lower abdominal region. Control the lowering phase.

Russian Twist

3 Sets 20 Reps Obliques

Seated, lean back slightly, rotate torso side to side. Targets obliques for a defined waistline.

Cable Crunch

3 Sets 15 Reps Upper Abs

Kneeling with cable overhead, crunch down. Allows progressive overload on abs unlike regular crunches.

Mountain Climber

3 Sets 30 sec Cardio Core

In plank position, alternately drive knees to chest. Great cardio and core exercise combined.

Treadmill HIIT

20 min High Intensity Fat Burn

Alternate 30 sec sprint / 60 sec walk. Burns more fat in less time than steady-state cardio.

Cycling

30–45 min Low Impact Endurance

Low-impact cardio that's easy on joints. Great for recovery days or longer steady-state sessions.

Jump Rope

10–15 min Full Body Coordination

Burns ~200 calories in 15 minutes. Improves coordination, timing, and cardiovascular fitness rapidly.

Rowing Machine

20 min Full Body Power

Works 86% of muscles in the body. Drive with legs first, then back, then arms. Perfect full-body cardio.

Burpees

3 Sets 10–15 Reps HIIT

Squat down, jump feet back to plank, push up, jump up with arms raised. One of the most calorie-burning exercises.

Gym Knowledge Hub

Expert tips and essential knowledge for your fitness journey

01 🔥

Warm Up Always

Always spend 5–10 minutes warming up before intense exercise. Dynamic stretches, light cardio, and mobility drills prevent injury and improve performance.

02 💧

Stay Hydrated

Drink at least 3–4 liters of water daily. During workouts, drink 200–300ml every 20 minutes. Dehydration reduces performance by up to 20%.

03 😴

Rest & Recovery

Muscles grow during rest, not during workouts. Get 7–9 hours of quality sleep. Take at least 1–2 rest days per week for optimal recovery.

04 🥩

Protein Intake

Consume 1.6–2.2g of protein per kg of body weight daily. Best sources: eggs, chicken, paneer, dal, whey protein. Spread intake across all meals.

05 📈

Progressive Overload

Gradually increase weight, reps, or sets over time. This is the fundamental principle for continuous muscle growth and strength gains.

06 🧠

Mind-Muscle Connection

Focus on feeling the target muscle working during each rep. Research shows this increases muscle activation by up to 35% compared to mindless lifting.

07 📐

Proper Form First

Never sacrifice form for weight. Wrong technique leads to injuries. Master the movement with lighter weight before adding load to your exercises.

08 🍽️

Pre & Post Workout Nutrition

Pre-workout: complex carbs + protein 1–2 hours before. Post-workout: fast-digesting protein + simple carbs within 30 minutes after training.

09 ⏱️

Workout Duration

Optimal workout sessions are 45–75 minutes. Longer sessions lead to hormonal decline and diminishing returns. Quality beats quantity every time.

10 🎯

Set Smart Goals

Set SMART goals: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound. Track your workouts in a journal or app for continuous improvement.

11 🔄

Vary Your Workouts

Change your routine every 4–6 weeks to prevent plateaus and keep motivation high. Try different exercises, rep ranges, and workout styles.

12 🚫

Avoid Overtraining

Signs of overtraining: fatigue, poor sleep, declining performance. Listen to your body. Consistent moderate training beats sporadic intense training.

💡 Nutrition Rules

🥚 Best Protein Sources in India

Eggs, chicken breast, paneer, Greek yogurt, dal (lentils), chickpeas, whey protein, tofu, fish (tuna, salmon). Aim for 20–30g protein per meal.

🚫 Foods to Limit

Refined sugar, white bread, fried foods, packaged junk food, excessive alcohol, sugary drinks. These slow recovery and promote fat storage.

💊 Supplements (Optional)

Creatine monohydrate, whey protein, vitamin D3, omega-3 fish oil, and magnesium are well-researched and safe. Always consult our trainer before starting.
